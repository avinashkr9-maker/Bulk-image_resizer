How to Resize Images for Amazon Product Listings in 2025

8 minute read · June 2025 · Updated for current Amazon requirements

If you sell products on Amazon, you already know that product images are one of the most important factors in whether a customer clicks on your listing or scrolls past it. But beyond the creative quality of your photos, Amazon has strict technical requirements for image dimensions, file formats and background colours. Failing to meet these requirements can result in your listing being suppressed or your images being rejected outright.

In this guide, we will cover exactly what Amazon requires for product images in 2025, why these requirements exist, and how you can prepare hundreds of product photos at once without spending hours in Photoshop.

Amazon Main Image Requirements

Amazon has two categories of product images: the main image and additional images. The main image has the strictest requirements because it is the image that appears in search results and is the first thing a customer sees.

Requirement Specification Why It Matters

Important: What is NOT allowed on the main image
  • No text, logos, watermarks or graphics overlaid on the image
  • No lifestyle photography showing the product in use
  • No coloured, gradient or patterned backgrounds
  • No mannequins for clothing items in most categories
  • No borders, frames or decorative elements

Amazon Additional Image Requirements

Amazon allows up to 9 images per product listing, with the main image plus up to 8 additional images. The additional images have more flexibility in terms of backgrounds and content.

For additional images, you can use lifestyle photography showing the product being used, infographic images with text explaining product features, comparison charts, size guides, and images showing the product from different angles. The same size requirements apply, with a minimum of 1000 pixels on the longest side and a recommended size of 2000 x 2000 pixels for consistency.

Why Amazon Requires 2000 x 2000 Pixel Images

Amazon introduced its zoom feature to allow customers to get a closer look at products before purchasing. When a customer hovers over a product image on desktop, Amazon automatically zooms in to show greater detail. For this zoom feature to work properly without the image becoming blurry or pixelated, Amazon needs the original image to be at least 1000 pixels on the longest side.

However, Amazon strongly recommends 2000 x 2000 pixels because modern computer monitors and mobile devices have high pixel density displays. An image that looks sharp on an older monitor may appear slightly soft on a Retina or 4K display. Starting with 2000 x 2000 pixels ensures your product looks crisp and professional on every device.

How to Prepare Your Product Images for Amazon

Step 1: Remove the background

Amazon requires a pure white background for main product images. If your products were photographed in a studio on a white background, you may be able to use the images directly. However, most product photos have backgrounds that are slightly off-white, grey, or have shadows that do not meet Amazon's requirements. Use a background removal tool to remove the background completely, then replace it with pure white.

Step 2: Resize to 2000 x 2000 pixels

Once you have a clean product image with a transparent background, resize it to 2000 x 2000 pixels. The product should be centred and fill at least 85% of the frame. If your product is not square, use the Contain fit mode which will place your product in the centre and fill the surrounding space with white background.

Step 3: Save as JPEG

Although Amazon accepts PNG files, JPEG is recommended because JPEG files are smaller and upload faster. Save your image as a high quality JPEG at 90% quality. This gives you a file that looks excellent while being small enough to upload quickly.

Step 4: Name your files correctly

Amazon recommends naming your image files using your ASIN or product identifier followed by a variant code. For example, B07XY12345.MAIN.jpg for the main image and B07XY12345.PT01.jpg for the first additional image. Consistent file naming makes it much easier to manage your product catalogue.

Common Mistakes Amazon Sellers Make with Product Images

  • Images that are too small: Submitting images below 1000 pixels means customers cannot use the zoom feature, which reduces conversion rates significantly.
  • Off-white backgrounds: A background that looks white on screen may not be pure white (RGB 255, 255, 255). Amazon's automated systems can detect this and suppress your listing.
  • Product too small in frame: If your product does not fill at least 85% of the image frame, Amazon may reject it. Make sure your product is centred and fills most of the canvas.
  • Wrong aspect ratio: Amazon requires square images (1:1 aspect ratio) for the best display. Non-square images may be automatically cropped in ways that cut off parts of your product.
  • Blurry or low resolution images: Starting with low resolution photos and trying to enlarge them always results in blurry images. Always start with the highest resolution photo you have.

Summary: Amazon Image Requirements Checklist

  • Size: 2000 x 2000 pixels (minimum 1000 x 1000)
  • Background: Pure white (RGB 255, 255, 255) for main image
  • Format: JPEG recommended, PNG also accepted
  • Product fills at least 85% of the image frame
  • No text, logos or watermarks on main image
  • No lifestyle photography for main image
  • Quality: 90% JPEG quality or higher

Following these requirements consistently across all your product images will ensure your listings are never suppressed due to image issues and will give your products the best possible visual presentation to customers browsing on Amazon.
