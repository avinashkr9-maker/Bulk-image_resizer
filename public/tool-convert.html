Why Convert Image Formats?

Different image formats serve different purposes. JPEG is the most universal format and works everywhere including email, print and all devices. WebP is the modern web format that offers 25-35% smaller file sizes than JPEG at equivalent quality, making it the best choice for websites and ecommerce stores in 2025. PNG is a lossless format ideal for logos, graphics and any image that requires a transparent background.

Converting your images to the right format for each use case can significantly improve website performance, reduce storage costs and ensure compatibility across all platforms and devices.

Frequently Asked Questions
Should I convert all my images to WebP?

Yes, for websites WebP is the best format in 2025. All modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge support WebP. It loads faster and uses less bandwidth than JPEG or PNG, directly improving your page speed scores and SEO rankings.

Will converting to WebP reduce image quality?

At 85-90% quality, WebP images are visually indistinguishable from JPEG originals while being significantly smaller in file size. The default quality setting of 90% in our tool provides excellent results for all use cases.

Can I convert PNG to JPEG?

Yes. Note that PNG supports transparency while JPEG does not. If your PNG image has a transparent background, it will be converted to white when saved as JPEG. If you need to preserve transparency, keep the image as PNG or convert to WebP which also supports transparency.
