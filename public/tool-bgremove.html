How Does AI Background Removal Work?

Our background remover uses RMBG-1.4, an advanced deep learning model developed by BRIA AI specifically for background removal tasks. The model has been trained on millions of images and can accurately distinguish foreground subjects from backgrounds even in complex scenarios with similar colours, detailed edges, and partially transparent objects.

Unlike older edge-detection algorithms that struggle with hair, fur, or complex backgrounds, RMBG-1.4 uses semantic understanding to identify what the main subject of an image is, regardless of what surrounds it. The entire process runs in your browser using WebAssembly and ONNX Runtime, which means your images are never uploaded to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why does it take time on first use?

On your first visit, the AI model file (approximately 40MB) needs to download to your browser. This happens once and is then cached automatically. On subsequent visits and for all following images in the same session, processing is much faster.

What types of images work best?

The tool works best on product photos, portraits and images where there is a clear subject against a distinct background. Plain or studio backgrounds give the best results. Complex backgrounds with many overlapping elements may produce less precise edges.

Can I replace the background with a custom colour?

Yes. After background removal you can download the image with a transparent background, white background, black background or any custom colour of your choice using the colour picker in the settings panel.
